An ***essay*** is (definition:: an [[artifact\|artifact]] that results from [[writing\|writing]], usually a few hundred to several thousand [[word\|words]], usually focused on one or just a few [[topic\|topics]].)

Here's a listing of various types of essays:

{ .block-language-dataview}

- [[expository essay\|Expository essays]] are for [[informing\|informing]] people about a [[concept\|concept]] or [[topic\|topic]].
- [[descriptive essay\|Descriptive essays]] are for [[describing\|describing]] a [[thing\|thing]], often focusing on one [[enc/def/quality\|quality]] or set of qualities.
- [[narrative essay\|Narrative essays]] are for [[narrative storytelling\|narrative storytelling]].
- [[argumentative essay\|Argumentative essays]] are for [[arguing\|arguing]] a [[truth\|truth]] about a [[thing\|thing]].
- [[Analytical essay\|Analytical essays]] are for [[relating\|relating]] a [[concept\|concept]] or [[topic\|topic]] to [[event\|events]] or [[information\|information]].
- [[comparative essay\|Comparative essays]] are for looking at what is similar and different between two or more [[thing\|things]].
- [[critical essay\|Critical essays]] apply a [[concept\|concept]] or [[synthesis\|synthesis]] of concepts to events or information.
- [[reflective essay\|Reflective essays]] explore my [[relationship\|relationship]] to a [[thing\|thing]].

Check the [[Essay Catalog\|Essay Catalog]] if you want to see all the essays that are a part of [[emsenn's Webplace\|my virtual mycelium]].
