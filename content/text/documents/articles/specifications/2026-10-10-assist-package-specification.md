# ASSIST

## What this is

ASSIST is the **Agent-Supported Service Interface Simulating Topologies**. It is one package, downstream of TUFTE (the Tiling User Form Topology Engine), and it is the shared engine of every "* Assist" app the 561 Group has or plans. Each Assist app is served at its own host under `assist.561.group`: `eve.assist.561.group`, `gig.assist.561.group`, `alpaca.assist.561.group`, `redshirt.assist.561.group`, `muse.assist.561.group`, `kalshi.assist.561.group` and every later one.

ASSIST serves the Group's micro-SaaSes to humans the way x402 serves them to agents. The x402 market sells a qualifying service's typed operation to an agent over HTTP payment; ASSIST serves the same operation to a person as an interface they can study, operate and pay for. One service declaration feeds both listings.

This specification is the first carrier of ASSIST as a package. It does not replace the Assist application platform specification of 2026-09-11 (v0.3.0) or the muse-assist specifications of 2026-09-27. It states how both are realized as one package downstream of TUFTE, and it is the specification the requirement `assist-shared-engine-downstream-of-tufte` asks for. The requirements of the 561 Group service that turns a qualifying service into an Assist app are in `services-become-assist-apps` and in the specification `2026-10-10-assist-app-qualification-and-conversion-service.md` in `561-group-services-section`.

## The name, read part by part

- **Agent-Supported.** An agent works beside the human in every Assist app. In the 2026-09-11 platform this is the bring-your-own-key analyst loop and the remote MCP endpoint through which the person's own assistant calls the same tools; in muse-assist it is the continuing-work assistant with human authority over effects (muse-assist requirements R-06). The human makes every decision; the agent retrieves, cites and proposes (EVE Assist's principles: decision support never automation; provenance on every retrieved fact; retrieval separated from inference; the assistant may say the data is insufficient).
- **Service Interface.** The thing served is a service: a typed operation with a domain type, a codomain type and declared effects, the same declaration the x402 catalog entry carries (`compute`, `validate`, `bodySchema`, `output.schema`). ASSIST is the interface to it, never a second implementation of it.
- **Simulating.** The interface lets the person run the service on hypotheticals before committing: Kalshi Assist's What-if forks from Holofoam ("if the NYC high moves two degrees, how does the field re-glue?"), a pacing report over a hypothetical week, a grow plan over a hypothetical schedule. A simulation is an execution of the same operation with its effects withheld, recorded as such.
- **Topologies.** The interface is a form topology in TUFTE's sense: tiles and typed forms with a stable reading order, neighbourhoods and live updates. A service's domain type becomes a TUFTE form; its codomain becomes tiles; linked services (Kalshi's linked markets, the brackets of one event) become neighbouring sites in one field.

## Place in the house

ASSIST is seated at `computer-web-engineering-services-section`, beside TUFTE (`apps/web-page-tiling-engine`), because it is a web application engine downstream of TUFTE. Its package directory, when fabricated, is `computer-web-engineering-services-section/apps/assist`, named `@red-cup-engineering/assist`. That directory is where the requirement's conditions look for its SAS and its dependency on TUFTE.

Its upstream, in dependency order:

1. **TUFTE** (`@red-cup-engineering/web-page-tiling-engine`): tiles, typed forms, topology inspection, pure reducers returning receipts and refusals, `tufte:action`/`tufte:receipt`/`tufte:refusal`/`tufte:projection` browser events, `createTufteApplication` returning a Fetch handler that Node and Workers both call. ASSIST carries no copy of any of it.
2. **The web lineage** that TUFTE composes: `computer-web-platform/application`, `computer-web-server`, and the computer platform.
3. **The Assist engine of 2026-09-11** (`@red-cup-engineering/personal-intelligence-systems-services-section`): the Assist application record, principals and login adapters, the signed session, sealing, the store over D1, the analyst loop, the MCP endpoint over the record's operations, the glossary tool, and `sync-assist-public`. These capabilities move into ASSIST or are imported by it from their canonical owners; ASSIST never keeps them in two places.
4. **The x402 catalog declaration** (`x402-services-section/data/market-561-group/records/catalog.json`): the one declaration of a priced operation. ASSIST reads a service's declaration from it; it never redeclares one.

Downstream of ASSIST are the Assist apps. Each is a parameterization: an Assist instance record plus a domain section's operations, login adapter and knowledge records. No Assist app carries engine code.

## Host-neutral by construction

The 2026-09-11 engine is a Cloudflare Worker (`createAssistWorker`); muse-assist is a MetroPlat instance on Node (`metroplat-serve`). These are two faces of one handler, not two engines. TUFTE's application runtime already returns a handler that "accepts a standard Request" and that Node serves through `startWebServer` and Workers call "from their native fetch adapter". ASSIST inherits this: `createAssist(instance)` returns one Fetch handler; the Worker seat and the MetroPlat instance are its two faces. A session that finds an Assist app running a third host face migrates it onto one of these two.

## The Assist instance record

An Assist instance is one data record admitted against ASSIST's schema. It extends the 2026-09-11 Assist application record (brand, login, tools, systemInstruction, buildToolCtx, routes, requiredCapabilities, subrequestBudget) with:

- `host`: the instance's host, `<name>.assist.561.group` or a deeper label under it;
- `parent`: the instance this one specializes, or none. `cannabis.grow.assist.561.group` names `grow.assist.561.group`; `indoor.cannabis.grow.assist.561.group` names `cannabis.grow.assist.561.group`. A child instance inherits every field of its parent and overrides only what it names. The host label chain mirrors the parent chain;
- `services`: references to x402 catalog entries (by route) or to domain operations the instance serves to its person;
- `topology`: the TUFTE workspace into which those services project (which operations are forms, which results are tiles, which services are neighbours);
- `simulation`: which operations may run with effects withheld, and how a simulated result is labelled;
- `listing`: the human listing record of the instance (see below);
- `legacyHosts`: hosts the app served on before ASSIST (`eve-assist.561.group`, `alpaca-assist.561.group`, `kalshi-assist.561.group`, `rail-assist.561.group`, `redshirt-assist.561.group`, `muse.561.group`), each answered by a permanent redirect to `host`.

The brand lives in this one record and nowhere else (directive brand-in-one-record, 2026-09-06).

## The human listing beside the x402 listing

The x402 market lists a priced operation for agents as one catalog entry: name, gateway, section, operation, route, method, price, category, schemas, examples, framings, ledger account, markets, resources, compute and validate exports. ASSIST lists the same operation for humans in the same form. A human listing is a record under `561-group-services-section/data/records/listings/` that names the x402 entry it stands beside (by route), carries the same `compute` and `validate` exports, and adds the fields only a human surface needs: the Assist host, a `human` framing (title, description and tags written for a person), the login kinds accepted, the human price and its ledger account (`Income:Sales:Assist:<gateway>`), and the TUFTE topology it is presented in. The first such record is `2026-10-10-pacing-assist-listing.json`.

Because both listings derive from one declaration, a change to the operation changes both, and an operation is never sold to agents and humans under two different contracts.

## High-level design

| Component | Responsibility | Owner it composes |
|---|---|---|
| Instance admission | Admit an Assist instance record, resolve its parent chain, refuse with the offending field | computer-data-engine's evaluator |
| Service binding | Resolve each `services` reference to its x402 catalog declaration or domain operation; declare it as a capability-effect operation | x402 catalog; typed-computer-code-engine |
| Topology projection | Project each operation's domain type into a TUFTE form and its codomain into tiles; lay linked services out as neighbours | TUFTE |
| Simulation | Execute an operation with effects withheld; label the result as simulated; keep simulated and committed results apart in the store | computer platform's execute |
| Principal and session | Login adapters (EVE SSO, EVM wallet, workforce identity, email), signed session, sealed credentials | 2026-09-11 engine; web-server cookies; cryptographic section |
| Agent support | Analyst loop over the same operations; MCP endpoint for the person's own assistant | 2026-09-11 engine; MCP-over-HTTP service |
| Listing | Emit the human listing of each instance from its declaration | this specification's listing form |
| Hosting | One Fetch handler; Worker face and MetroPlat face; legacy-host redirects | TUFTE runtime; MetroPlat; Cloudflare Workers |

The fabrication order is the house's: these requirements and this specification, then a SAS, an HLD and an LLD under `apps/assist/content/text/documents/articles/specifications/`, then code by codegen from the admitted schemas and by codemod migrating the 2026-09-11 engine's consumers onto ASSIST.

## Alignment with the Group's method

The Leader names ASSIST as a way "to align code with our best theory". The alignments it carries:

- **Praxis-driven and market-driven development.** ASSIST is built because a market exists for its apps (micro-SaaSes for humans) and is judged by use; an Assist app that no person uses is evidence, not success.
- **Rendanheyi operations around the Huidu Guanli hull.** Each Assist app is a micro-enterprise facing its own users, with its own listing and ledger account; the conversion service in the 561 Group is the platform that incubates them.
- **Requirement management and specification engineering.** ASSIST's requirements are held in `content/requirements` and its specifications follow the SAS, HLD, LLD chain.
- **Use-and-improve, meet-then-beat.** ASSIST realizes the 2026-09-11 engine and muse-assist rather than building beside them, and each Assist app names the real-world alternative it meets then beats (Kalshi's own market list for Kalshi Assist; the platform versions named in muse-assist requirement R-10).

## Requirements this specification states

- The ASSIST package shall depend on the TUFTE package and carry no copy of its engine.
- The ASSIST package shall return one Fetch handler per Assist instance, served by both a Cloudflare Worker face and a MetroPlat face.
- Each Assist instance shall be one data record admitted against the ASSIST instance schema.
- An Assist instance that names a parent shall inherit every field of its parent that it does not override.
- Each Assist app shall be served at a host under `assist.561.group` whose label chain mirrors its instance's parent chain.
- Each legacy Assist host shall answer with a permanent redirect to the app's host under `assist.561.group`.
- The ASSIST package shall project each served operation's domain type into a TUFTE form and its codomain type into TUFTE tiles.
- The ASSIST package shall execute a simulated operation with its effects withheld and shall label its result as simulated.
- The ASSIST package shall read a served operation's declaration from the x402 catalog entry when one exists, and shall carry no second declaration of it.
- Each human listing shall name the x402 catalog entry it stands beside and carry that entry's compute and validate exports.
