A TUFTE form is a visible, typed shape through which application state is
perceived, edited, validated, submitted, admitted or refused, and reconciled.
Native HTML forms are its accessible and progressively enhanced floor, not its
maximum expression. The term includes preservation of user input and focus
across updates. It is governed by the
[TUFTE framework charter](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/08/30/2026-08-30-note-on-tufte-framework-charter.md).
