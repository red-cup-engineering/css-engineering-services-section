The TUFTE engine is the coherent runtime and toolchain that turns user-form
topology into operable browser applications. Rendering is one engine duty;
updates, scheduling, resources, effects, transitions, diagnostics, testing,
and build/server cooperation are also engine duties. It composes upstream
engineering services rather than valuing a zero-dependency artifact above the
product mandate. The term is governed by the
[TUFTE framework charter](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/08/30/2026-08-30-note-on-tufte-framework-charter.md).
