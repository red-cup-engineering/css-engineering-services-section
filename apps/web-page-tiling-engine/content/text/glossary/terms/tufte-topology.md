TUFTE topology is the inspectable, transformable relation among application
surfaces, forms, authored and visual adjacency, focus, routes, data, and
actions. The current runtime exposes viewport-independent tile membership,
semantic placement, form ownership, and reading-order edges. Responsive and
nested relations remain to be implemented. The term is governed by the
[TUFTE framework charter](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/08/30/2026-08-30-note-on-tufte-framework-charter.md).
